This much-loved home has been extensively renovated, incorporating an extension and a full downstairs refurbishment in 2019 designed by interior designer Alannah Monks, creating a modern and inviting living space. The property also benefits from triple/double glazed windows and doors. The most recent update, a new high-spec family bathroom completed in 2024, reflects the continued care and attention given to the property over the years.
